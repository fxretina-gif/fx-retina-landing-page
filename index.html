FAQ

Questions clinic owners ask on the first call

What does it cost?

Management starts at ₹45,000 a month, plus your ad spend, which you pay to Google and Meta directly. Once a chain spends more than about ₹5 lakh a month on ads, we charge 10% of ad spend instead. On the call we'll work out what a paying patient should cost at each of your branches, so you can judge the fee against that.

How soon will we see patients?

Ads go live within 5 days of the onboarding call. Most clinics see footfall move in month 2, and month 3 is where it compounds. That's why we work in 90-day plans.

What do you need from my team?

One onboarding call to list every treatment and its price, access to your ad accounts, doctor photos and videos, and someone at each branch to confirm appointments. Each week your team shares walk-ins and revenue so we can report every branch properly.

Do you really call our enquiries?

Yes. Our in-house team calls every enquiry, checks the treatment, budget and location, and passes the qualified patients to the right branch. Your front desk gets ready patients instead of a list of phone numbers.

You're in Lucknow. Can you run clinics in Mumbai or Bengaluru?

We already do. We run clinics in Mumbai, Bengaluru, Coimbatore, Delhi NCR and Rajkot from Lucknow. Ads, pages and reporting are all remote, and your branch managers sit in one group with our ad managers.

Is there a lock-in?

We work in 90-day plans, paid in advance, because clinic results build over three months. After that, you renew month to month, so you stay only while the numbers work.

Do you work with my competitors?

No. We take one clinic per specialty in each area. If your area is already taken for your specialty, we'll tell you on the call.
