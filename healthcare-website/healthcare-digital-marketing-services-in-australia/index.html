Compliance First

AHPRA & TGA-aware marketing.

Advertising regulated health services in Australia is tightly governed. We build campaigns that respect AHPRA advertising guidelines (including the rules around testimonials for regulated services), stay mindful of TGA requirements, and handle patient data under the Privacy Act / APPs — so you grow without breaching the National Law.

FAQs

Australia healthcare marketing — your questions.

Is your marketing compliant with AHPRA advertising guidelines?

Yes. We design campaigns and websites within AHPRA advertising rules for regulated health services — including the restrictions on testimonials — and stay mindful of TGA requirements.

Which Australian healthcare providers do you work with?

Dental and orthodontic practices, dermatology and cosmetic/aesthetic clinics, physiotherapy, optometry, IVF and fertility, GP and specialist clinics across Australia.

How is patient data handled under Australian privacy law?

We build data capture and analytics in line with the Privacy Act and Australian Privacy Principles (APPs), with consent-aware forms and secure handling.

How do you generate patient enquiries for Australian clinics?

Local SEO and Google Business Profile, Google Ads on high-intent searches, fast conversion-focused websites, and call/form tracking to measure every lead.

How long does SEO take in Australian cities?

Local and technical SEO often show movement within 60–90 days; competitive Sydney, Melbourne and Brisbane terms typically take 4–6 months depending on competition and content.
