Compliance & Ethics

Ethical, compliant marketing.

Healthcare marketing in Canada must adhere to strict guidelines. We ensure every campaign respects patient confidentiality, avoids misleading claims, follows provincial healthcare regulations and maintains ethical advertising standards.

Compliance First

PIPEDA & CASL-aware marketing.

Canadian healthcare marketing must respect privacy and anti-spam law. We build data capture and analytics in line with PIPEDA (and provincial equivalents), keep email and outreach compliant with CASL, and follow provincial college and CMA advertising standards — so your growth is fast and defensible.

FAQs

Canada healthcare marketing — your questions.

Is your Canadian healthcare marketing PIPEDA and CASL compliant?

Yes. We handle patient data in line with PIPEDA and provincial privacy law, and keep email and outreach within CASL consent rules, alongside provincial college advertising standards.

Which Canadian healthcare providers do you work with?

Dental and orthodontic clinics, dermatology and med-spas, physiotherapy, chiropractic, optometry, fertility clinics, cosmetic and private practices across Canada.

Can you market in both English and French?

Yes. We can build and optimise English and French pages and campaigns for Quebec and bilingual markets to reach patients in their preferred language.

How do you get Canadian clinics more patients?

Local SEO and Google Business Profile, Google Ads for high-intent searches, conversion-focused websites, and call/form tracking so every enquiry is measured to source.

How long does SEO take in Canadian cities?

Local and technical SEO often move within 60–90 days; competitive terms in Toronto, Vancouver and Montreal typically take 4–6 months depending on competition and content.
