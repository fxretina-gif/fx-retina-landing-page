FAQs

UK healthcare marketing — your questions.

Do you work with private clinics and NHS practices in the UK?

We focus on private and self-pay healthcare — private GPs, dental practices, aesthetic and cosmetic clinics, dermatology, ophthalmology, fertility, physiotherapy and private hospitals — where digital marketing directly drives patient enquiries and bookings.

Is your healthcare marketing GDPR and ASA compliant?

Yes. We build campaigns and websites with UK GDPR and ICO guidance in mind for data capture and consent, and we follow ASA/CAP rules for advertising claims in healthcare and cosmetic services.

How much does healthcare digital marketing cost in the UK?

It depends on your goals, competition and location (London and the South East are more competitive). Most UK clinics start with a focused SEO or Google Ads retainer and scale as leads and bookings grow. We provide a custom quote after a free strategy call.

How long does SEO take to rank a UK clinic?

Local SEO and technical fixes often show movement within 60–90 days, while competitive terms in cities like London, Manchester and Birmingham typically take 4–6 months to reach the first page, depending on competition and content.

Can you generate leads for UK medical tourism and self-pay treatments?

Yes. We run patient-acquisition campaigns for self-pay treatments such as cosmetic surgery, dental implants, hair transplants and fertility, including UK patients travelling abroad and international patients coming to UK clinics.
