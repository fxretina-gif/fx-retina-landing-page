FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Why should healthcare businesses invest in PPC advertising?

PPC (Pay-Per-Click) advertising helps healthcare providers reach potential patients instantly through targeted ads on search engines and social media platforms. It generates qualified leads, increases appointment bookings, and improves online visibility.

What makes FX Retina Healthcare one of the best PPC agencies for healthcare in India?

FX Retina Healthcare specializes in healthcare marketing, creating customized PPC campaigns that comply with healthcare advertising guidelines while focusing on patient acquisition, lead generation, and ROI-driven results.

How does healthcare PPC differ from regular PPC advertising?

Healthcare PPC requires specialized keyword research, compliance with medical advertising policies, patient-centric messaging, and precise audience targeting. FX Retina Healthcare understands these unique requirements and develops campaigns accordingly.

Which healthcare providers can benefit from PPC services?

Our PPC services are ideal for hospitals, clinics, eye care centers, dental practices, IVF centers ,PCOD treatment, cosmetic surgeons, diagnostic labs, medical tourism companies, and individual healthcare practitioners.

How long does it take to see results from healthcare PPC campaigns?

Healthcare PPC campaigns can start generating traffic and leads almost immediately after launch. Most clients begin seeing measurable improvements in inquiries and appointments within the first few weeks.

What platforms does FX Retina Healthcare use for PPC advertising?

We manage campaigns across Google Ads, YouTube Ads, Facebook Ads, Instagram Ads, LinkedIn Ads, and other digital platforms to maximize healthcare marketing performance.

How does FX Retina Healthcare choose the right keywords for PPC campaigns?

Our team conducts detailed keyword research based on patient search behavior, treatment-specific queries, local search intent, and competition analysis to attract high-converting traffic.

Can PPC help healthcare businesses attract patients from specific cities or regions?

Yes. We create location-targeted PPC campaigns that help healthcare providers reach potential patients in specific cities, states, or regions across India and internationally.

How does FX Retina Healthcare measure PPC campaign success?

We track key performance indicators (KPIs) such as clicks, impressions, conversions, appointment bookings, cost per lead, return on ad spend (ROAS), and overall campaign ROI.

Does FX Retina Healthcare provide PPC services for medical tourism businesses?

Yes. We specialize in PPC campaigns for medical tourism companies, helping them attract international patients seeking quality and affordable healthcare services in India.
