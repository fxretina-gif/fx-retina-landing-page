FAQs

US healthcare marketing — your questions.

Do you offer HIPAA-conscious healthcare marketing in the USA?

Yes. We build data capture, tracking and websites with HIPAA and patient-privacy best practices in mind, and follow Google and Meta healthcare advertising policies so your campaigns stay compliant while they grow.

Which US healthcare providers do you work with?

We work with hospitals, multi-location clinics, dental and orthodontic groups, dermatology and med-spas, ophthalmology, orthopedics, fertility clinics and private practices across the United States.

How do you generate patient leads for US clinics?

Through a full funnel: local SEO and Google Business Profile for organic visibility, Google Ads for high-intent searches, conversion-focused landing pages, and call and form tracking so every lead is measured back to source.

How long does SEO take to work in competitive US markets?

Technical and local SEO often show traction within 60–90 days. Competitive city and treatment terms usually take 4–6 months, depending on your market, website condition and content investment.

Do you serve providers nationwide or by city?

Both. We run city and ZIP-level local campaigns for single-location practices and multi-location strategies for groups and hospital systems across the US.
